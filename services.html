The services

Every service at the Club is done the traditional way — unhurried, precise, and finished properly. Here's what the chairs are for.

01 — Signature

Turkish Shave

The service the shop is known for. A close, careful straight-razor shave in the traditional Turkish style — the kind of shave that leaves the skin smooth for days, not hours.

  • Hot towels before the blade touches skin
  • Rich lather worked in with a brush
  • Cool towel finish to calm the skin
02 — Ritual

Hot-Towel Treatment

Steam is the oldest trick in the barber's book. Hot towels soften the beard, open the pores and turn an ordinary shave into twenty minutes of pure rest.

  • Steamed towels applied and re-applied
  • Prepares skin for the closest possible shave
  • Can be added to any cut or shave
03 — Precision

Skin Fades

Clean, seamless blends from skin to length — cut with sharp clippers, refined with scissors on top, and finished with a razor-crisp hairline.

  • Seamless blending, no visible lines
  • Razor-sharp edging and line-up
  • Styled to finish before you leave the chair
04 — Detail

Beard Styling

A beard is a frame for the face — it deserves more than a trim. We shape, sculpt and line up beards of every length, edged with hot lather for a clean finish.

  • Shaped to suit your face
  • Hot-lather edging for crisp lines
  • Tidy-up between full cuts
Ask in the shop about junior cuts and full cut-and-shave combinations — the barbers will talk you through the options and book you in at the chair.
