The craft

A ritual, not a rush.

Turkish barbering is a craft handed down through generations. Every shave at the Club follows the same unhurried steps — because the ritual is the point.

  1. 01

    The steam

    Hot towels soften the beard and open the pores, so the blade glides instead of dragging.

  2. 02

    The lather

    Rich lather worked in with a brush, lifting every hair and cushioning the skin beneath.

  3. 03

    The razor

    A steady hand and a sharp straight razor — slow passes, stretched skin, total control.

  4. 04

    The finish

    A cool towel closes it all down, leaving the skin calm, smooth and fresh.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Where exactly is the shop?
You'll find us inside Morrisons Supermarket at 30 Hagley Road, Birmingham B16 8PE — in the Five Ways area. Just head into the store and follow the signs.
Do I need to book, or can I walk in?
You can book through Booksy or send the shop a message on Facebook to reserve a chair. Calling 0121 454 2107 ahead is the quickest way to check availability.
What are your opening hours?
We're open Monday to Saturday, 9am to 8pm, and Sundays 10am to 5pm — seven days a week.
Do you do traditional hot-towel shaves?
Yes — the hot-towel shave is our house specialty. Steamed towels, rich lather and a straight razor, finished the traditional Turkish way. See the services page for the full ritual.
